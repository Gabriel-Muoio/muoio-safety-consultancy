Services

Health and safety support suited to the work.

An engagement may involve a particular document or decision, a review of work in the field, or a broader examination of a problem that has been difficult to resolve internally.

01

Safety advice and problem-solving

Independent advice on recurring incidents, critical risks, disputed controls or parts of a safety system that are not working as intended.

02

Risk and work-method support

Development or review of SWMS, risk assessments, procedures and critical-control material, with attention to how the work will actually be performed.

03

Review and implementation support

Targeted audits, incident and corrective-action reviews, consultation, training and practical support to put agreed improvements into effect.

Scope

Engagements can range from a focused piece of safety work to a broader review of recurring incidents, ineffective corrective actions or gaps between formal systems and work as performed. If you are unsure what kind of support is appropriate, get in touch and describe what you need.

When independent advice may help

Some matters are straightforward—others need a closer look.

  • You need help preparing or reviewing a SWMS, risk assessment or procedure.
  • A project or change in work needs practical safety input before it begins.
  • The same type of incident or control failure keeps returning.
  • Corrective actions have stalled or are being closed without confidence.
  • A concern has become difficult to examine objectively from inside the organisation.
