Critical control
verification.

Use this template at the point of work to check whether a critical control is present, effective and likely to remain effective.

01

Verification details

Record where and when the check took place.

02

What matters

Keep the event, control and required standard specific.

03

Check the control

Verify through observation, conversation and evidence—not assumption.

04

Outcome and action

Choose one outcome. Act immediately if the control is not effective.
